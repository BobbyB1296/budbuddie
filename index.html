Why We're Building Bud Buddie

Cannabis should be easier to understand. Bud Buddie is a community-driven cannabis discovery platform focused on helping people find products, learn what works for them, discover deals, and make more confident purchasing decisions. We’re building it alongside the people who know the industry best.

What We're Learning

  • Finding the right cannabis product can feel overwhelming.
  • Customers want recommendations they can trust.
  • People want alerts when favorite products go on sale.
  • Education matters just as much as product availability.
  • Every cannabis experience is personal.
  • Human interaction should always remain an option.
