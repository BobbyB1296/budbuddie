Our Mission

Bud Buddie is a community-driven project exploring how technology can help cannabis consumers discover products, learn about cannabis, find deals, and navigate dispensaries with confidence.

What We're Learning

  • Customers want personalized recommendations.
  • People want alerts for deals and favorite products.
  • Some users prefer human interaction over automation.
  • Dispensaries want better customer education.
  • Trust and transparency matter.
